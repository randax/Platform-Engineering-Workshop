New database

One knob: a T-shirt size. It bundles compute, storage and HA replicas (small · 1  /  medium · 2  /  large · 3 instances) — you pick intent, the platform decides the rest.

What is actually being created? (View YAML)

The UI form is just sugar. It submits the following YAML to the Kubernetes API:

apiVersion: platform.cloudbox.io/v1alpha1
kind: WorkshopDatabase
metadata:
  name: my-db
  namespace: {{.Namespace}}
spec:
  size: small
